## What this adds to the plan

1. **Phone orders today are the reverse of the decided flow.** Dispatch is immediate, the SMS carries a tracking link, the payments seam has no authorise step, and the state machine has no payment-pending state. This is the largest single change in the epic, and it touches every surface.
2. **After booking, the rider app shows only a status line and a cancel button.** That one gap covers R5.2, R5.3, R5.9, R5.10 and R5.11. A rider trip card plus a receipt closes most of MK 541 p. 8.
3. **Licence data, approval reasons and the carrier entity are one missing data model** behind C1, C2, C3 and R5.2. The #309 gate is enforced well; it just checks nothing yet.
4. **Two recorded decisions are reversed:**
   - `earnings-screen.tsx`'s "Q1 = Option B" (no per-ride history) must go, because R4 requires 3-month history.
   - The phone form's "card is unsettleable at the kerb" comment no longer holds once card is the only method.
5. **New lawyer question:** does a server-computed fixed quote satisfy "the driver calculates the fare in the app" (40. (13) 1))? This is R2. The GPS fixes queued while offline (R1) also need a legal view.
6. **The offline rule needs no rework.** Nothing can complete without the server.
